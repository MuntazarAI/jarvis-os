# Code of Conduct

JARVIS-OS is a respectful, technically focused project.

Participants should be respectful and constructive, discuss code and ideas
rather than attacking people, welcome good-faith technical disagreement, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberate disruption.

Security reports and private project information must be handled through the
project's private reporting channels.

Maintainers may remove or reject interactions that violate this code or
seriously disrupt the project.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any shared channel where someone is taking part
in the project — and when an individual is representing the project in
public.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and give people the benefit of the doubt before
  escalating.
- Be specific about the code or the idea. Critique the work, not the person.
- Accept that "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are useful answers.
- Keep discussion proportionate: a preference is not a defect, and a
  disagreement about architecture is not a conflict about people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, or identifying a person for
  harm.
- Submitting contributions that bypass the project's policy, approval, or
  security layers — including attempts to make the assistant execute
  unauthorised actions.

## AI-assisted contributions

Contributions written with AI assistance are welcome. They are held to the
same standard as any other contribution, and the author is responsible for
the code they submit. Please say so in the pull request when a tool
generated a substantial part of the change.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issues, and comments that do
not align to this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
participant for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or
offensive. Decisions are made in the project's interest, and contributors
are told what happened.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules fairly and consistently, and
to prioritise the safety and dignity of people over the convenience of the
maintainer.
